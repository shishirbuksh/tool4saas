## 2. Tool Page Content Architecture (135 Tools)

Every published tool adheres to the 12-point quality checklist:
1. **Unique Title Tag**: Formatted with brand suffix (e.g. `Word Counter — Tool4SaaS`).
2. **Unique Meta Description**: 140–160 characters describing primary utility benefits.
3. **Single Clear H1**: Matches the tool name and intent directly.
4. **Useful Introduction**: 2–3 sentence TL;DR explaining what the tool does and its privacy guarantee.
5. **Interactive Functional Surface**: Blade template loaded cleanly in `<x-tool-page-shell>`.
6. **Step-by-Step Instructions (HowTo)**: 3 to 4 sequential numbered cards explaining usage.
7. **Real Examples & Technical Specs**: Concrete inputs and outputs demonstrating accuracy.
8. **Relevant FAQs**: 3 to 6 high-intent questions and detailed answers.
9. **Related Tools ("See Also")**: Contextual chips linking to tools within the same category.
10. **Educational Guide Callouts**: Bidirectional links connecting tools to their corresponding in-depth blog pillar.
11. **Breadcrumb Navigation**: Semantic `<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">` linking `Home > Category > Tool`.
12. **Structured Data**: JSON-LD `WebApplication`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage`.
